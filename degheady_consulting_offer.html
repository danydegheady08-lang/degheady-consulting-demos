The Problem

Your team is doing work
that should not
require a human.

Every high-performing sales and finance team has the same problem. The work that drives revenue and insight is buried under hours of manual administrative tasks that exist only because no one has replaced them yet.

01
Sales reps lose 30 to 60 minutes per call to post-call admin
Writing follow-up emails, updating CRM records, drafting proposals by hand. Across a team of five, that is up to five hours of selling time lost every day to work AI handles in seconds.
02
Deals go cold because follow-ups are delayed or impersonal
When a rep waits two days to follow up, the prospect has moved on. Speed and personalization are not nice-to-haves. They are where deals are won or lost.
03
Monthly financial reports take days to produce manually
Hours spent extracting numbers from accounting software and building board summaries from scratch every month. The same output can be produced from the same data in under a minute.
04
Manual processes do not scale with team growth
What works for a team of five becomes the ceiling for a team of twenty. The admin load that was manageable becomes the thing that stops growth.
