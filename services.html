What We Build

Two systems.
Built around how you already work.

Not off-the-shelf software you configure yourself. Every deployment is built around your actual CRM, your actual reporting format, and your actual team, then run by us for as long as you need it running.

I.

AI Sales Assistant

Follow-Up & CRM Automation

Converts raw post-call notes into a ready-to-send follow-up email and a complete, structured CRM deal summary. The rep spends the time they used to lose to admin actually selling instead.

The problem this solves

A rep finishes a call and now owes two things: a follow-up email that actually references what was discussed, and a CRM entry that's accurate enough for the next person to pick up the deal cold. Most reps do one of those well and rush the other, or delay both until end of day, by which point the moment that made the follow-up compelling has already passed. Multiply that by every call, every rep, every day, and it's a genuine tax on selling time, not a minor inconvenience.

What's Included

  • Follow-up generation — a specific, referenced email drafted within seconds of the call ending, not a template with names swapped in
  • CRM deal summary — structured fields populated automatically: deal stage, next steps, key concerns raised, decision-maker identified
  • Tone matching — configured to sound like your team, not like an obviously automated message
  • Deal risk flagging — calls out when a deal shows signs of stalling based on language used

Who It's Built For

  • B2B sales teams doing 4 or more calls per rep, per day
  • Recruitment agencies managing high call volume with candidates and clients
  • Teams where follow-up speed is a known factor in win rate
  • Any team currently relying on a rep's memory and end-of-day admin time
II.

Financial Reporting AI

Board-Ready Reporting

Converts raw financial data into a board-ready executive report every single month, consistent formatting, real variance analysis, forward-looking commentary, regardless of who is available to build it that week.

The problem this solves

Most 10 to 50 person companies don't have a dedicated FP&A function. Reporting falls to whoever has the least on their plate that month, built from scratch in a spreadsheet, with commentary written under time pressure right before a board meeting. The report's quality becomes a function of who happened to build it, not a reliable, repeatable standard. That inconsistency is the actual risk, not the hours spent, though those add up too.

What's Included

  • Executive summary — written in board-appropriate language, not raw data dumped into a template
  • KPI dashboard — the metrics that actually matter for your business, not a generic template
  • Variance analysis — month-over-month and against plan, with the actual drivers named
  • Forward-looking commentary — a real read on trajectory, not just a restatement of history

Who It's Built For

  • Founder-led companies without a dedicated finance hire yet
  • Teams reporting to a board or investors on a monthly or quarterly cycle
  • Finance managers spending 3 or more hours per cycle on manual report-building
  • Any team where report quality currently depends on who's available that month
A Note On Demos

We don't put a live tool
in front of a stranger.

Every deployment is configured to a client's specific CRM and reporting format during setup, which means a generic public demo would either be misleading or genuinely broken depending on what you clicked. We'd rather show you the real thing, live, on a call, using your own data, where we can walk through exactly how it connects to your systems. That's not a sales tactic, it's the more honest way to demonstrate something that's built to be specific to you.
