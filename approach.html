How We Work

From first call
to live system, in a week.

No lengthy discovery process, no six-week implementation. Every engagement follows the same real sequence, built for speed without skipping the part that actually matters, understanding how your team works before we build anything.

The Process

Two phases.
One relationship.

Every engagement has a setup phase, where the system gets built and tested, and a retainer phase, where we keep it running and improve it. Here's exactly what happens in each.

Step 1

A 20-minute consultation call

We ask about your current workflow, how follow-ups and reporting actually happen today, what tools you use, and where the real friction is. No pitch deck, just questions, because the answer to whether this is a fit depends entirely on your specific situation, not a generic pain point.

Before Setup
Step 2

Workflow mapping

We map your actual process end to end, which CRM fields matter, what a good follow-up looks like for your team specifically, what your board actually wants to see in a report. This is the step most tools skip entirely, and it's the reason a generic template usually falls short.

Setup, Week 1
Step 3

Build and configure

The system gets built against your actual CRM or data source, not a demo environment. Tone, format, and logic are configured specifically to what we learned in the mapping step, not left as generic defaults you'd have to adjust yourself.

Setup, Week 1
Step 4

Testing against real data

Before anything goes live, it's tested against real calls or real financial data from your business, not synthetic examples. You review the actual output before the system touches anything your team relies on.

Setup, Week 1
Step 5

Launch

The system goes live. Your team starts using it immediately, typically within a week of that first call, not a month of back-and-forth implementation.

End of Setup
Step 6

Ongoing operation

We monitor it, fix anything that breaks, and refine it as your team's needs shift. Monthly check-ins keep it aligned with how your business is actually evolving, not frozen at how it worked on day one.

Retainer, Ongoing
