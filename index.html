The Problem

You hired closers,
not administrators.

Recruitment firms and SaaS sales teams share the same problem. Every conversation generates admin. Follow-ups, CRM entries, candidate summaries, monthly reports. It is all done manually, and it is costing you deals, placements, and hours you will never get back.

01
Your reps spend more time logging deals than closing them
Post-call emails, CRM updates, candidate summaries. 30 to 60 minutes per call, per rep. Across a team of five that is five hours of revenue-generating time lost to admin every single day.
02
Slow follow-ups are costing you placements and deals
The rep who follows up within the hour wins. The one who follows up two days later, after writing the email from scratch, loses to someone faster. Speed is not optional in sales or recruitment.
03
Your finance team rebuilds the same report every single month
Same structure. Same process. Same hours wasted extracting, formatting, and writing commentary that could be generated automatically from data that already exists.
04
This gets worse as your team grows, not better
Manual workflows that barely work at ten people collapse at twenty. The admin load that felt manageable becomes the ceiling on your growth.
